01 / Privacy

Your information.

This notice covers this portfolio website and enquiries or meeting bookings you make with Nusa & North. Reuben Hester, trading as Nusa and North, is responsible for the personal information we use for those purposes. Our business and contact details are below.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, name and anything you choose to send, such as a project brief, company details or telephone number. If you book a call through Calendly, we receive the booking and contact information you provide. Please only send information relevant to your enquiry.

We use this information to answer you, arrange a conversation and discuss or deliver a project. Where you are asking us to enter a contract with you, we rely on the steps needed before that contract or its performance. For general enquiries and contacts acting for a business, we rely on our legitimate interest in communicating with people about our services. Where records must be kept to comply with law, the basis is that legal obligation.

Contact details are needed to reply; you do not have to send a project brief to browse the portfolio. This website has no newsletter sign-up or online checkout.

When you browse or watch a film

We do not install separate visitor analytics scripts or advertising pixels on this version of the website. It remembers your media choice and, if you choose a portfolio layout, your Reel or Grid preference. Details are in Cookies & media.

Delivering a webpage involves technical information such as an IP address, requested URL and browser details. The public website uses GitHub Pages for hosting; GitHub may process request information for delivery, security and abuse prevention. GitHub’s privacy statement explains its processing.

Some film posters are delivered by Vimeo or YouTube image servers. Your browser contacts those servers to retrieve the image, which shares request information such as your IP address and browser details. Locally hosted images do not make those third-party requests. Our legitimate interest is displaying the work in this portfolio.

Vimeo and YouTube players load only after you allow external media. They receive technical and playback information when loaded. Your consent is the basis for allowing optional media through our website. You can refuse and keep browsing, or change your choice in the media settings on this website.

Services involved

  • Google: our Gmail correspondence and YouTube films or poster images. Google privacy policy.
  • Vimeo: film hosting, players and some poster images. Vimeo privacy information.
  • Calendly: scheduling when you follow our booking link. It opens on Calendly’s website; it is not embedded here. Calendly privacy notice.
  • GitHub: hosting of the public website, as described above.

Information may also need to be shared with a professional adviser or authority where necessary for a legal obligation or legal claim. Our social links open other websites, whose own notices explain their processing.

International processing

These services operate internationally, and information may be processed outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Their linked privacy notices describe the locations and safeguards that apply to their services, including applicable adequacy arrangements or standard contractual clauses. Contact us if you would like more information about the safeguards relevant to information we handle.

How long information is kept

Enquiry and booking information is kept for as long as needed to handle the conversation and relevant follow-up. If an enquiry becomes a project, relevant correspondence forms part of that project record. Retention then depends on the work, any ongoing contractual obligations, applicable accounting or tax requirements, and the time for establishing or defending legal claims. We keep information only while one of those purposes requires it.

Our media choice lasts six months; the portfolio layout preference remains until you or your browser clear it. Third-party providers apply their own retention periods to information they control, as explained in their notices.

Your rights and choices

Depending on the circumstances, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of your personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time; this does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Email nusaandnorth@gmail.com to exercise a right or ask a privacy question. We may need reasonable information to confirm your identity. We will respond within the time required by data-protection law, normally one month. This website does not make decisions about you through automated profiling.

You can also complain to Ireland’s Data Protection Commission or another competent supervisory authority. You do not need to contact us first to do so. The DPC explains your data-protection rights.

02 / Cookies & media

Your browser. Your choice.

We use browser storage for the two preferences below. Local storage is similar to cookies in that information stays on your device, although it is not automatically sent with every website request. We do not install separate visitor analytics or advertising pixels on this version.

NamePurposeDuration
nn-media-consentRemembers whether you allowed or declined external video players and when that choice expires. This stores a choice, not a browsing history.Six calendar months, then a new choice is required.
nn-portfolio-viewRemembers the Reel or Grid layout you select in the portfolio.Until cleared by you or your browser.

External video players

Vimeo and YouTube players are optional. They remain blocked until you allow external media, including the moving previews inside the social phones. You can allow or decline media without losing access to the rest of the website. A separate link lets you watch on the provider’s own website instead.

Vimeo uses its dnt=1 option and YouTube uses its privacy-enhanced embed domain. These settings reduce tracking; they do not make playback anonymous or stop all transfers to the provider. Vimeo also notes that existing Vimeo cookies can still be sent by your browser.

Vimeo identifies security cookies that can still be used with its DNT setting: player_clearance (7 days), cf_clearance (1 year), _cf_bm (30 minutes), and _cfuvid (the browser session). Which are set depends on the request and browser. See Vimeo’s current cookie table and YouTube’s privacy-enhanced player information for their current behaviour.

Change or withdraw your choice

Use media settings to allow or decline external players at any time. Declining stops the players on this website from loading and removes any currently open embedded players. It does not undo information already sent to a provider or remove cookies stored on that provider’s domain; you can clear those through your browser settings.

Clearing this site’s storage removes remembered preferences. If storage is unavailable, your choice may last only for the current page. Clicking a plain Calendly, Instagram, Vimeo or YouTube link takes you to that service, where its own settings apply.

More about the rules: Irish DPC guidance on cookies and similar technologies.
